Deep analysis

The statistical work behind the recommendations: how strongly Steam player counts and Twitch viewership actually correlate, what a Random Forest model says drives viewership, and whether the scoring logic used elsewhere on this site actually predicts which games are about to rise. Generated straight from EDA.py — global totals, plus a breakdown per month. Click any game (a point, a bar, or a stacked segment/legend entry) to highlight that game across every chart on this page; click it again, or the chip that appears, to clear it.

Week and Day only apply within a specific month — pick a Month first. Weeks are calendar weeks within that month (Week 1 = days 1–7, and so on). Leave Week on "All weeks" and the Day dropdown lists every day of the month; pick a Week and Day narrows to just that week's days. Some charts (like the feature-importance model) need more data than a single day gives and won't show for that granularity — try Week or the whole Month instead.

Timing signals

Streamer activity, viewer engagement, game market share and the viewer-per-streamer ratio, all broken down by day/hour — filterable by clock reference and by broadcast region so you can see whether the pattern holds for a specific audience, not just the global average. Two of these charts also have a view toggle for a second dimension (which games make up the totals) alongside the simple view.

Region is estimated from each stream's broadcast language, not verified viewer location — treat it as a rough proxy, not a precise geography.
