Potential score is NOT the same metric as the 0-100 Opportunity score used on the Call Sheet: it standardizes Twitch viewer_count and Steam current_players into z-scores (how many standard deviations each snapshot is above/below that period's average across ALL games) and blends them 60/40 in favor of viewers, per game/day snapshot, then averages per game. Because it's z-scores, it's centered on 0 and can go negative (below-average) — it flags titles whose viewership AND player counts are unusually high relative to everything else tracked that period, not just titles with the biggest raw audience.
