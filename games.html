How is the Opportunity score calculated, and what do the colors mean?

Each game's Opportunity score (0–100, shown top-right of every card) blends three normalized signals: audience size (40%, average viewers), viewers-per-streamer (35%, how under-saturated the game is — more viewers for every person streaming it means less competition), and recent growth (25%, first-half vs. second-half of the tracked window, clipped to −100%–300% so one outlier week can't dominate). Each signal is scaled against the 5th–95th percentile across all games before blending, then the whole thing is discounted toward zero for games backed by fewer than ~15 distinct streamers, so a single person's lucky night can't fake a high score.

66–100 — strong opportunity 33–65 — moderate 0–32 — low / saturated

The mini day-of-week board inside each game's detail card is a small heatmap: it colors each day by that game's average viewers, scaled 0–5 against its own busiest day (not the whole site's scale). Faint mint = this game's quietest day; solid glowing mint = its single best day. It's a per-game view of the same idea as the site-wide Signal Board.

Methodology. Opportunity score blends audience size (40%), viewers-per-streamer (35%) and clipped growth (25%), then discounts games backed by fewer than ~15 distinct streamers so a single person's lucky night can't dominate the ranking. Best day/hour is shown in whichever mode you pick inside a game's detail card — standardized (UTC) or time-of-day (streamer-local, estimated from broadcast language).
