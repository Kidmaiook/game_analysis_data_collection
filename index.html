How is "how crowded it is" calculated?

This card looks up the current UTC day and hour in the same viewers-per-streamer ratio used on the Signal Board (standardized/UTC view), then ranks that ratio against every other tracked hour: what percentile is right now in, compared to the rest of the week?

Top third (rank > 66%) — Wide open Middle third (33–66%) — Moderate Bottom third (rank < 33%) — Crowded

The progress bar fill is that same percentile rank (0–100%), not the raw ratio itself — so it's always relative to this dataset's own week, not an absolute viewer count.
