About Us

We built this platform for our graduation project, driven by data analytics and our passion for gaming content strategy.

Green Room (Data-Driven Gaming Trend Analysis) is a senior graduation project developed at the College of Computing, Prince of Songkla University (Phuket Campus).

As gamers and creators ourselves, we noticed a common challenge: many independent content creators rely on intuition or short-lived hype when picking games to stream. This often leads to wasted time and low audience engagement. Green Room bridges this "information asymmetry" by analyzing real-time metrics from Steam and Twitch to help creators make data-driven decisions.

Project Objectives

  • Steam vs. Twitch Correlation: Investigate whether player counts on Steam have a statistically significant relationship with viewer counts on Twitch.
  • Data-Driven Dashboard: Develop an interactive analytics dashboard to evaluate high-potential games and reduce the risk of choosing games purely by instinct.
  • Key Performance Indicators: Identify and weight the key variables that drive a game's reach and success on streaming platforms.

Data Sources & Technology

Our system ingests, cleans, and models data from multiple sources including the Steam API, Twitch API, SteamSpy, SteamCharts, and SullyGnome using automated batch ETL pipelines.
